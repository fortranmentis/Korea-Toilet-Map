Korea Toilet Map

The nearest public restroom in Korea, right now.

Find any of 80,000+ public and open restrooms across South Korea on a map, see which ones are open now, and get directions. Built for both walking and driving, and fully available in English for travelers.

Download free on the App Store

Features

  • Nearest open restroom

    One tap finds the closest restroom that is open right now.

  • Opening hours

    Understands 24-hour, daytime, and overnight schedules and separates open from closed places.

  • Walking & driving modes

    On foot, see restrooms around you. Driving, see options ahead of you ranked by route distance.

  • Apple Maps & Naver Map

    Start walking or driving directions to the restroom you picked.

  • Grade filters

    Show Gold, Silver, or Bronze facilities, or only places that are open now.

  • Six languages

    Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and Arabic.

FAQ

Where do I find a public toilet in Seoul or Busan?

Open the app, tap "Nearest restroom", and it finds the closest open public restroom from your location anywhere in South Korea.

Is it free?

Yes, the app is free on the App Store.

Does it work offline?

The restroom data is bundled in the app, so nearby search runs on your device. Map tiles and directions need an internet connection.

Is my location sent anywhere?

No. Your location is used only on your device and is never sent to a server.

Restroom data: Public Data Portal of Korea (data.go.kr). Actual conditions may differ on site.
